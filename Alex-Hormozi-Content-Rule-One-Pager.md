# The 10-to-1 Content Rule — One Pager

> ## Give 10 reasons to do **1 thing** — not 1 reason to do 10 things.
> One clear action. Ten angles of persuasion.

---

### The numbers
| | |
|:--:|---|
| **10→1** | 10 reasons, 1 action |
| **2** | levers: **motivation** + **ability (ease)** |
| **3** | content types: **stories · stats · explanations** |
| **4** | value wrapper: big outcome → **fast · easy · risk-free** |
| **5** | write at a **5th-grade level** |

---

### The job of content
A piece of content is a **motivating operation** — a short-term nudge to act now.
Pull one of two levers:
- **↑ Motivation** — give the *reasons* (the why).
- **↑ Ability** — make it feel *fast, easy, risk-free* (the how).

### The 3 vehicles for your reasons
- **Stories** — real testimonials *or* hypotheticals (equal power). "See someone like me win."
- **Stats** — numbers that back the claim.
- **Explanations** — *how it works*; this is what breaks a limiting belief.

### Add "flavor" (the value equation)
For every reason: **big dream outcome**, made **fast**, **easy**, **risk-free** (cheap for B2B).
**Speed sells:** speed = stories = stats = explanation.

---

### Operating principles
- **Clarity wins.** 5th-grade level reaches the most people and forces you to be clear — faster to make, faster to consume.
- **"Don't talk about stuff — talk about how to *do* stuff."** Teach the how-to.
- **Education = influence + a positive outcome.** → **Selling = Stories + Stats + Explanation + Value Equation.**
- **Volume de-risks.** Ship ~10 pieces; more shots = higher odds one overperforms = 10x output.

---

### Explain, don't describe
| ❌ Description | ✅ Explanation |
|---|---|
| "He stole because he's dishonest." | "He stole because he was rewarded for it before." |
| "He stole because he stole." (says nothing) | Names a real **cause**. |

*Most people give descriptions dressed up as explanations. Give the real cause.*
