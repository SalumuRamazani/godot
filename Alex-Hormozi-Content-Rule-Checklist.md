# Content Checklist — The 10-to-1 Rule

Run a piece of content through this before you publish. The goal:
**10 reasons → 1 action.**

---

## ① Pick the 1 thing
- [ ] I chose **one** action or belief for this piece (not several).
- [ ] A cold viewer could actually **do or accept it right now**.
- [ ] If I have more than one ask, I split it into separate pieces.

## ② Stack the 10 reasons
- [ ] I listed up to **10 reasons** to do that one thing.
- [ ] Each reason **increases the good** (bigger outcome) **or decreases the bad** (less risk/effort/cost/fear).

## ③ Choose the vehicles (stories · stats · explanations)
- [ ] At least one **story** — real testimonial *or* hypothetical (both work).
- [ ] At least one **stat** that backs the claim.
- [ ] At least one **explanation** of *how it works* that breaks an old belief.

## ④ Wrap each reason in the value equation
- [ ] Names a big **dream outcome**.
- [ ] Shows it's **fast**.
- [ ] Shows it's **easy**.
- [ ] Shows it's **risk-free** (and **cheap**, for B2B).
- [ ] I led with **speed** somewhere — "do X, get the result faster."

## ⑤ Clarity pass
- [ ] Reads at a **5th-grade level** — a kid could follow it.
- [ ] Shows **how to do stuff**, not just talks *about* stuff.
- [ ] Every "because" is a **real cause (explanation)**, not a **relabel (description)**.

## ⑥ Output
- [ ] This is one of ~**10 pieces**, not a one-off — volume finds the winners.

---

### ⚠️ The trap to avoid
**Description ≠ explanation.**
- ❌ "He stole because he's dishonest." (relabel)
- ✅ "He stole because he was rewarded for stealing before." (real cause)

If your "why" just restates the "what," rewrite it.
